Ignore (`POST /inventory/devices/{id}/ignore {ignored}`, operator, audited `inventory.ignore` / `inventory.unignore`) keeps the row, so the device's identity follows the existing rules (MAC, agent key, never the address) and a re-sighting is the same ignored device. An ignored device: (1) is out of To review, the badge, the Devices list (an Ignored tab and `?ignored=true` list it) and every count, Home's included; (2) sends no new-device alert, and its open new-device incident closes "Closed by Vedette: the device was ignored by <person>."; (3) gets no network scan and no SNMP read: the scan policies read both D-601 per-device sources as off while it is ignored, but the stored choice (an admin's off or on) and the unknown mark are kept and apply again when it is watched (turning a source on while ignored is 409 `device_ignored`), and it is left out of the Scan now picker; (4) is outside every vulnerability reader (`MatchInput`, `AssetIDs`, `DevicesRunning`), so no finding is made for it; its **open** findings close as not relevant with the reason "Closed by Vedette: the device was ignored by <person>." and no notice, while findings a person already settled (patched, mitigated, false positive, not relevant, risk accepted) keep their status, note and history (nothing is deleted; a risk acceptance of an ignored device does not expire until it is watched again, so no alert); it and its findings are left out of the weekly and PDPL reports (Ignore's closes are never "resolved"); the AI and MCP device lists mark it `ignored`; the hourly findings reconcile repairs a lost ignore or un-ignore event either way, and a match or scan result that raced the Ignore writes nothing (checked inside the write). A join (automatic or a person's Merge) keeps an approved device a person watches over an ignored row, and never hides it: an automatic join keeps the watched approved row (a row added by hand still stays first, keeping its origin and typed address), and when the kept row is ignored (a join of fixed direction, or the person's choice in Merge) it is watched again; otherwise the kept device keeps its own state. The audit row and the Merge dialog say which state survives, and the moved findings follow the kept device (closed on an ignored one, reopened on a watched one if Ignore had closed them). **Security exception:** trap hits (honeypot) and intrusion-detection / log incidents never ask about the device, so they still alert for an ignored device: a staff phone touching a trap is exactly what the customer must hear. Stop ignoring (the Ignored tab or the device page) sends the device through review again: not approved, its own scan and SNMP settings in force again, **only the findings Ignore closed reopen** (a person's decisions stay as they were), then the device is matched again as usual; no notice for the close or the reopen. A phone with a private (locally administered) MAC may come back under a new MAC as a new device; the Ignore confirmation says so; no MAC-randomisation feature. An osquery enrolment never approves an ignored device.
