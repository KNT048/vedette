Tiers 2–3 live on their own page, "Updates to plan", one item per update, not in the Findings list.** (1) **Threshold 0.1 on the EPSS probability**, not a percentile: FIRST's own comparison of strategies uses "EPSS of 0.1 and higher" against "CVSS 7 and higher", and at 10 % the EPSS v3 study reports about 63 % coverage of exploited CVEs at about 65 % efficiency while asking to fix about 2.7 % of CVEs (Jacobs et al. 2023, as summarised by FIRST's material and the community guide below); the probability means the same thing every day while a percentile moves with the whole population. Measured 2026-11-14 (api.first.org, `epss-gt`, `percentile-gt`): ≥ 0.05 → 31,039 CVEs, **≥ 0.1 → 17,562**, ≥ 0.2 → 10,244, ≥ 0.5 → 4,387; top 5 % (percentile ≥ 0.95) → 20,332, about EPSS 0.08. 0.05 doubles the list (noise for a view meant to be read); 0.2 or more drops a third of what 0.1 catches. CISA's SSVC has no EPSS cut-off of its own (its Exploitation point is none / PoC / active, which is tier 1), so FIRST's example is the reference.
