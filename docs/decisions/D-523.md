At most `SweepProbes` = 17 packets an address behind a router (nmap stops once it answers); on the box's own Ethernet segment nmap still sends one ARP request and none of these. `--source-port 53` from the same chapter is left out: an evasion trick, not a defender's. `sweepWarning` now names both costs (one packet an address on the segment, up to 17 behind a router) and warns when the routed cost does not fit the window. (2) **Normal**: adds `--osscan-limit` (OS detection only with one open and one closed port; otherwise the device shows no OS rather than a wrong guess) and `--max-retries 3`. **Versions** gets `--max-retries 3` too: the reason (nmap's default allows 10 retransmissions, `-T4` 6; a LAN loses few, and a lossy Wi-Fi client stretches a run) is the same, and fewer retries are gentler on a device of unknown kind; it keeps no `-O`, so no `--osscan-limit`.
