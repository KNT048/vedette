| D-628 | **A domain's ownership proof covers its subdomains, may sit at `_vedette-verify.<name>`, is answered at the asked name itself (never through a CNAME or a wildcard), never crosses a public suffix, and is one rule for core and the executors (exposure, J6, kien G10; amends D-229, D-591 and exposure INV-1 (c), INV-6).** (1) The record `vedette-verify=<token>` of a domain target, found on that domain, proves it and every name under it, at any depth: a verified beyondnetglobal.com covers git.beyondnetglobal.com with no second record. The check of a target (Check, and again right before every scan, INV-1 (c)) asks for the target's own record, then the record of each domain target of this box on a parent name, down to the registered domain (at most 8 names). The record counted at a domain is only that domain's own target's token: never another target's token placed on a parent. That is the executors' rule, so core and they cannot disagree. (2) `_vedette-verify.<name>` also takes the record, for a name that is a CNAME, where DNS allows no other record (the provider answers "Record could not be added"). It is not offered when it would pass 253 characters. (3) A proof is accepted only when it is answered at the asked name itself. The name must not be a CNAME, else the TXT belongs to whoever runs the CNAME's target (a dangling or vendor CNAME). A record on `_vedette-verify.<name>` must not be a wildcard: a random name beside it must not answer the same value. A wildcard `*.zone` CNAME is refused by the CNAME rule and a wildcard TXT by the sibling rule (review r1 High of #941). (4) A proof never sits on or crosses a public suffix of the Public Suffix List (golang.org/x/net/publicsuffix, already linked), and **both** of its sections count. ICANN's suffixes (com, co.uk) are one boundary. The private ones (ddns.net, github.io, herokuapp.com) are the other: many customers register under them, so with the ICANN section alone a record on ddns.net would cover every customer's name under it. A public suffix is never a target (400 `bad_value`), and a stored one is refused as such. (5) One rule: `core/domainfence.Owned`, applied by exposure (Check, the scan gate), by the scan container's worker (deep steps) and by vedette-host (ZAP pages on both the box-bound and the older token path, OpenVAS). The scan of a child covered by a parent sends the parent's domain and token (`ScanTarget.Proof`, `DomainScope.Domain`) for the executors to check, the page's name staying under it. (6) One box is one organisation: the targets of two users of the box cover each other, and the audit names the user of each check. Revocation: a parent's record removed, or the parent target removed from the box (its token goes with it), covers no more at the next check, which runs right before every scan. A parent removed while a check runs counts for nothing in that check. A child added before its parent was verified is verified in the same transaction as the parent, with its own check message and audit row. (7) Scope does not widen: a scan of a target still lists and scans only names under that target (crt.sh, DNS, the 300-name rule of D-229), and `ScannableIP` and the IP commitment are unchanged. (8) A failed check names the names asked and what came back: no TXT record, another TXT value (cleaned and bounded, untrusted), a CNAME to another name (with where to put the record instead), NXDOMAIN, a timeout, a record refused as reached through a CNAME or a wildcard, and the box's domains above it that would cover it. | kien 2026-11-17 (G10): git.beyondnetglobal.com is a CNAME, GoDaddy refused the TXT, and the box said only "DNS lookup failed"; he asked whether subdomains need proving again.
