| D-434 | **The Sources page shows what a device must trust for syslog over TLS: on a default box the box's own CA (with its SHA-256 fingerprint, expiry and a PEM download), not the server certificate (S-LOG-1; closes the deferred line of `feat-logs-syslog-tls.md`; corrected after review r1).** Facts read first (D-262, D-351, `cmd/vedette/boxca.go`, `tls.go`): the 8443 certificate the syslog TLS port also serves is a leaf signed by the box CA (`CN=Vedette box CA`, 20 years, made once, kept in `core_secret`); the leaf is re-issued on renewal and on an address change (D-139), the CA is the stable part, and an owner's `-tls-cert` replaces both. So a device that imports or pins the leaf breaks at the next renewal, and a leaf has `CA:false`, so a FortiGate importing it as a trusted CA fails chain verification. Decision: `GET /logs/sources` carries `box.tls` = `{ kind, issuer, port, fingerprint_sha256, subject, not_after, pem }` (null/absent when the box serves no TLS, i.e. when `tls_unavailable` applies); `pem`, fingerprint, subject and expiry describe the certificate to import. `kind` is `box_ca` when the leaf is signed by the box CA (the PEM is the CA's, the same certificate `GET /intake/ca.pem` serves; cmd/vedette hands it in as `logs.Config.TrustAnchor`, the agent-intake trust anchor, so no core package changes), `self_signed` when the leaf is its own issuer (the PEM is the leaf), and `issued` when another authority issued it (the PEM is the chain the owner gave the box; the card names the issuer). Only CERTIFICATE blocks are ever kept (an owner's combined file may hold a key). While the leaf is being re-issued under a new CA the trust anchor answers 503: the card is then hidden for a minute rather than wrong. `port` is the port a device sends to (6514 on a default box, p+1 for another `-syslog-port`). The service asks the same `GetCertificate` a handshake would, so a renewed leaf shows at once; the PEM is public data, so no new route is needed and the GUI builds the download from it in the browser (no new outgoing flow, nothing for PRIVACY.md). The card tells the admin to import the CA as trusted and not to pin the server certificate; the device setup dialog of a TLS source points to it.
