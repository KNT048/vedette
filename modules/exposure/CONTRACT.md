# Exposure Contract

> Tier: optional · Story: S-EXP-1, S-EXP-2 · Last verified: 2026-11-21 (every statement checked against the code)

Source of behaviour: `web/src/features/exposure/{contract,mocks,data}.ts`, LEGAL §3.

## Invariants
- **INV-37** (rows): what is exposed is rows, never a document of one scan. `exp_host` (a name under a domain, its source and CNAME), `exp_address` (an address of a name, or the IP target itself), `exp_service` (an open TCP port of an address: `state` open or closed) and `exp_issue` (anything an engine reports: surface, nuclei, zap, openvas, match; `state` open, gone or handled) each carry `first_seen` and `last_seen`. The scan writer (`writer.go`) is their one writer: a scan updates `last_seen` of what it saw and keeps `first_seen`. Each scan records, as items of its run, which ports it probed on each address it reached (`checked_all`: every port, the IP target or a deep-scanned or box-scannable address; `checked_list`: the runner's common ports when it says it did not probe them all; `checked_top`: those common ports and the ports the default scan's service step probed there, with one run item of kind `port` (`ip:port`) for each open service outside the common ports it probed; `checked_web`: 80 and 443 by name). A service the scan probed and did not find is missed, not closed (INV-22): it is closed when the scan before that probed it missed it too (its `last_seen` is older than that scan's start), or at once when its address left the domain; a service seen again after one miss was never closed and is not "opened". A name whose lookup failed keeps its rows. The issues of a service that closes are gone with it. Otherwise an issue is gone only when its engine ran to the end where it is in this run, and no longer reported it: nuclei where that template ran on that port and found nothing (the scanner reported no_match: an error, a template nuclei printed nothing for, a run that never went out and the deep templates a scan fell back from give no such result), ZAP on the pages it scanned, OpenVAS on the addresses it finished (done; its key names the address: `<oid>@<ip>`) and their open ports, nmap's script findings on the addresses whose deep identification ran its scripts, a certificate's expiry when its name resolved and its service answered, matches on the services the comparison read, admin pages on the services this scan saw. An issue a person marked patched or mitigated that a scan reports again is open again (INV-40); accepted, not relevant and false positive stay handled. Exploited CVEs matched on the services are `match` issues, written at scan time. `exp_scan` keeps a run's mode and its summary (counts, checks, not-checked lines). Tests: `writer_test.go` (replay of the stored scans of a test network; two scans in a row close a vanished port and keep first_seen, one miss neither closes nor reopens it; the issues of a closed port go with it; a domain's deep engines make issues gone only on the addresses they reached).
- **INV-38** (runs): a scan is a run of the shared run manager (`core/run`), kind `exposure_scan`, lane `exposure`: queued in the database (one runs at a time on the box, the next starts when it ends, a restart keeps the queue and records a running scan as stopped by the restart). Scan now refuses a target with a scan queued or running (409 busy) and a target asked within `ScanEvery` (429); every scan is checked again right before it starts (the ownership proof, the box's own IP, the safety block), however short its wait, and its depth is the testing terms as they are then. The nuclei, ZAP and OpenVAS steps are steps of the run (Settings › Scanning's cards read the newest ended one), and what the deep scan did with each address is an item of the run (the rotation reads it). Every scan ends with exactly one `exposure.scan` audit row. Tests: `scanrun_test.go`, `TestContract_INV12_OneScanAtATimeAndNotTooOften`.
- **INV-39** (tree, `tree.go`): domain targets form a tree and every name under a domain is scanned by one target. (1) **Placement:** the parent of a domain target is the nearest other domain target that contains it (`proofat.Within`: never across a public suffix, never a name that only ends alike); an IP has none. `settleTree` is the one writer of `exp_target.parent_id` and `exp_host.child_target_id` (set only when the name is itself a target) and runs in the write of every change: a target added (`AddNode`, `AddDomain`: a subdomain goes under its apex, an apex added later takes the targets under it), removed (before its rows are deleted: its children move up to its parent and its names go to whoever scans them; `uncover` marks the last check of what its record proved as failed at once), edited (`EditNode`), and each scan's rows (`writeScan`). (2) **Scope** (`exp_target.scope_mode`, domains only, default `all`): `self` scans the name only and looks nothing else up; `all` every name found under it; `selected` the names chosen on its rows (`exp_host.selected`, `EditNode.Selected`: ids of its own names, at most 299 (one scan reads 300 names, its own included), only with that scope; otherwise 400 `bad_value`). (3) **Owner of a name:** of the enabled domain targets that contain it, the deepest whose scope takes it (its own name always); none takes it: nobody scans it. So a parent skips the name of an enabled child, and the names under a child whose scope takes them; a name under a child whose scope does not take it (`self`, or not chosen) is scanned by the parent when the parent's scope takes it. A scan reads its plan when it starts (`ScanTarget.Names`: `Scans`, `Find`, `Choose`, `Chosen`): the runner probes only the names it owns and lists the others without a lookup or a probe (`Content.Listed`: rows with name and source, no address); the deep gate gives no scope for a name the plan does not own. A change of the tree applies from the next scan start. (4) **The 300-name limit** counts only the names the scan reaches (children's names do not count for their parent); `self` asks no certificate log; `selected` on a domain with too many names to list scans the chosen ones and says so (`NotNamesCut`), and does not try the common names for names nobody chose (`NotWordlistChosen`: a name only that list finds is listed by a scan with scope `all`); `TooManyNames` says which scope to choose. (5) **Rows follow the owner** (`handOver`): when the target that scans a name changes (a child added, removed, switched off or narrowed), the old target's rows of that name go to the new one in the same write, merged with what it holds (first seen the earlier, the later reading wins, a person's decision on an issue kept): the name (a row in both), its addresses, the services on an address no other name of the old target points at with their issues (copied instead when the old target still reaches the address through another of its names), the issues about that name or address. Nothing is closed, reopened or made new, so a move raises no `opened` port and no new issue. A port or issue new in a scan's write stays opened/new once, in that scan's event, when the settle moves it to the target that scans it now; one folded into a row the receiver held before (`handLog.merged`) is not new. A name nobody scans keeps its rows as the last scan left them. (6) **Switch:** `exp_target.enabled` off: the gate answers `disabled` (409 `disabled` for Scan now and at run start; `ScannableIP` false; deep steps refused), a running scan of it is stopped, its names go to the target above it (with their rows) and come back when it is switched on. (7) **Proof through the tree:** the proof stays stateless and is looked for again right before every scan (INV-1 (c), INV-32): a node is covered when the record found is the own record, on its own name, of one of its ancestors: `exp_target.method` NULL and `last_check.via` names the ancestor; its own token anywhere, or another target's on a name above both (INV-32 (10)), proves it by itself (`dns_txt`). The ancestor's record removed, or the ancestor removed with its token, refuses the child's next scan. (8) Each scan records the names it probed as run items (kind `name`, the id of the name's row). Selfcheck `exposure.subdomain_target_under_its_parent` (pairs of a domain target and a domain target under it that is not among its ancestors) and `exposure.name_scanned_by_one_target` (names probed by the newest scans of two enabled targets, both started after both targets existed and after the last add, edit or removal of a target that contains the name) are 0. `TestTree_*`, `TestCount_SubdomainTargetsUnrelatedToTheirParentAndNamesScannedTwice` (`testdata/tree_targets.json`, anonymised), surface `TestDomainSkipsTheNamesItIsToldNotToProbe`, `TestNameCapCountsOnlyTheNamesTheScopeReaches`, cmd/vedette `TestExposureRunnerResult`.
- **INV-40** (issues handled, `issues.go`): an issue is handled in the findings vocabulary: `patched`, `mitigated`, `accepted` (with a last day from tomorrow to 365 days ahead, UTC days, else 400 `bad_until`; a last day with another resolution is 400 `bad_until`), `not_relevant`, `false_positive`; the three that leave the flaw need a note of at least 3 characters (400 `note_required`), a note is at most 500. `POST /exposure/issues/handle` handles the open ones among at most 500 ids and skips the others (counted), `POST /exposure/issues/reopen` reopens the handled ones; one audit row per issue (`exposure.issue_handle`, `exposure.issue_reopen`). An accepted issue opens again by itself once its last day is over everywhere (noon UTC the day after; job `accept-expiry`, audited by `system` with who accepted it until when). As in findings, a `patched` or `mitigated` issue that a later scan still reports is open again (the fix did not take): audited by `system` ("found again by a scan (was patched by …)") and alerted once; `accepted`, `not_relevant` and `false_positive` keep the person's decision whatever the scans say. Tests: `issues_test.go`.
- **INV-1**: No target is scanned unless all four conditions hold: (a) `status=verified`, (b) IP: not more than 30 days since the box last observed it (INV-4), (c) domain: the TXT record `vedette-verify=<token>` is still there (on the name itself, on `_vedette-verify.<name>`, on a parent name up to the registered domain with its own token, or on a parent target of the box that covers it, INV-32), **checked again right before every scan**, (d) the commitment has been recorded. (e) IP: the box still reaches the Internet through that IP (INV-13). A missing condition → `POST …/scan` returns 409 `not_verified|expired|txt_missing|ip_not_current` or 400 `commitment_required`; `blocked_reason ∈ {unverified, expired, txt_missing, no_commitment}` is the status label on `Target`. `TestContract_INV1_NoScanWithoutProof`
- **INV-2**: A public IP **found by the box itself** is only an IP that the box itself uses to reach the Internet (a typed-in IP is a second source, with its own evidence: INV-35): `box-ip/*` does not accept an IP; the box probes by HTTPS three public, independent "my IP" services fixed in the code (`checkip.amazonaws.com` (AWS), `icanhazip.com` (Cloudflare), `https://ifconfig.me/ip` (Google Cloud); fixed 2026-11-09: three different operators, the constructor refuses two URLs on the same host; no server of the project; not entered by the user, no reverse DNS, no following redirects, **no proxy** (`Transport.Proxy = nil`, even when the environment has `HTTPS_PROXY`: a customer's proxy would make all three services return the proxy provider's IP), default TLS verification, no User-Agent of its own (Go's default)). Each answer is plain text containing only one IP: trimmed of whitespace, parsed with `netip` (no zone, must be global unicast), at most 64 bytes, with a deadline; anything else is refused (fail closed). **The 2-of-3 rule**: one *round* asks each service once (each time on a new connection); only an IP that **at least two** services in that round return identically is accepted (one wrong service, or two services that disagree, is not enough). Over several rounds, **every IP that reached 2-of-3 in at least one round** is an IP the box sees (a company with several IPs behind load balancing: each new connection may exit through a different IP); a round with no agreement adds nothing. No round got an IP: `last_run.ok=false` (nobody answered, or the services disagreed), nothing added, nothing lost. Entering an IP into `POST /exposure/targets` → 400 `ip_not_allowed`, IPv6 too (every spelling: `2001:db8::1`, `[::1]`, `::ffff:8.8.8.8`). Every different public IP seen goes into the store (new or renewed). A *full* probe runs 5 rounds (15 requests, 5 per service); a *light* probe runs 1 round (3 requests). `TestContract_INV2_OnlyObservedIPs`, `TestContract_INV2_MultiIPDetect`, `TestProbe_*`
- **INV-3**: Refuse results that are private, reserved, CGNAT `100.64.0.0/10` and every address that is not IPv4 global unicast (400 `not_public` when every observed address is refused; a refused address never goes into the store). The RFC 5737 documentation ranges (192.0.2.0/24, 198.51.100.0/24, 203.0.113.0/24), 198.18.0.0/15 and IPv6 are also refused in the backend (the mock allows the documentation ranges only because sample data uses them); IPv6 is left for later. `TestContract_INV3_RefusesNonPublic`
- **INV-4**: An IP is valid for 30 days from the last time seen, renewing itself: each time the box observes an IP (an automatic probe, "Detect now", or `POST …/verify` of that very IP) then `verified_at = the time seen`, `expires_at = verified_at + 30d`; `validity ∈ {valid, expiring (≤ 5 days left), expired}`. An IP only expires when it is **not** observed for 30 days, that is, confirmation is only needed again when it cannot be renewed; an expired IP that is observed again becomes valid again (the old commitment stays). A domain has no `expires_at` (TXT is checked before each scan). The clock is injectable, no real sleeping in tests. `TestContract_INV4_RenewAndExpire`
- **INV-5**: Limits on confirmation by a person pressing (429 `rate_limited`). **Domain: one check every 30 seconds per target** (`DomainCheckEvery`; it is only a DNS lookup so it is short; in memory, forgotten on restart; the message says how many seconds remain: "This target was checked a moment ago. The next check is allowed in N seconds."; not using the box's hourly budget; `TestVerify_ADomainIsCheckedEvery30SecondsPerTarget`). **IP and Detect now: 10 times/hour/box** (they ask external public services, so the box's fuse is kept): `POST /exposure/box-ip/verify` and `POST …/verify` of an IP each count one; a probe by the auto-detect job does not count (the shortest schedule is 1 minute) and does not use up the user's attempts; Detect now and Verify again of an IP share one budget; the domain Check does not use it. When the attempts run out, `box_ip.retry_in_min` (in `GET /exposure/targets` and `PUT …/settings`; `null` when attempts remain) is the number of minutes (≥ 1) until the next attempt, so the GUI locks the Detect now button with "Try again in about N minutes" instead of only reporting it after a rejected click (`TestContract_INV5_SettingsSayWhenTheNextAttemptIsPossible`). Each one writes `exp_attempt` (kept 24 hours) and `core_audit`. At most 20 targets (400 `too_many`; auto-detect skips a new IP once there are 20 and says so in `last_run`); a duplicate domain → 409 `duplicate`. `TestContract_INV5_AttemptLimit`
- **INV-6**: An entered domain is only a domain name (no scheme, path, port, whitespace; a domain-name regex); wrong → 400 `bad_value`/`empty`. A public suffix (the Public Suffix List, both parts: co.uk, ddns.net, github.io) is never a target: 400 `bad_value` `ErrPublicSuffix`.
- **INV-7**: Commitment: only for an authenticated target (409 `not_verified|expired`). There is no separate route and no checkbox any more: the **first Scan now** by an **admin who has two-step login** records the commitment (`exp_target.commitment_by/at/version/sentence/user_id/method` + an audit row `exposure.commitment` in the same transaction, SYS-3): who, when, which target, and **exactly the sentence** shown next to the button (with the target name filled in) together with the sentence's version. When a commitment **of the current version** already exists it is not recorded again (any later scan by anyone). A commitment of an old version (or `version = null`) counts as unconfirmed for the current sentence: the gate blocks as if there were no commitment (`blocked_reason: no_commitment`, 400 `commitment_required` "The confirmation text changed…"), `commitment_notice` sends the new sentence, `Scan` (the schedule) and an operator are refused, and **the first Scan now by an admin after the upgrade replaces** the old commitment with the new version, with an audit row `exposure.commitment` "re-confirmed under the new sentence (replaces notice <old>)". Deleting a target deletes its result rows and commitment, not `core_audit`. A commitment recorded by an older version has `version/sentence = null`. `TestContract_INV17_*`, `TestContract_INV7_Commitment`
- **INV-8**: Results are rows (INV-37), not a per-day snapshot: `GET /exposure/targets/{id}/snapshots`, `…/snapshots/{date}` and `…/diff` remain in the contract and return `501 not_implemented` (results are read through `GET /exposure/tree/{id}/{hosts,addresses,services,issues,scans}`). `Counts.certs_expiring` counts certificates expiring within 30 days (`CERT_WARN_DAYS`).
- **INV-9**: Data that goes out only consists of the box's own IP sent to Shodan InternetDB when an admin presses (INV-21, PRIVACY flow 15), the authenticated domain name (searching for subdomains in the CT log: one search at `crt.sh` per scan of a domain, asked again once on error), the scan itself to the authenticated target under LEGAL §3 (`scan` INV-12: port scanning only on the box's authenticated IP; a domain's names are only checked for a web page by name on port 80 and 443, except the deep scan INV-28 when the domain's terms have been accepted: to every public IPv4 address that names under the domain resolve to, including hosting, CDN or third-party proxies) and an HTTPS request to the three "my IP" services of INV-2 (PRIVACY flow 9: the first time on install, then every auto-detect interval, each probe 3 requests (light) or 15 (full, at most once an hour); carrying no customer data; no server of the project). Turning the module off ⇒ both stop. Turning auto-detect off ⇒ flow 9 only runs when a person presses.
- **INV-10**: Response data from an external host (banners, page titles) and the responses of IP services are untrusted (SYS-6): size limits, stored as text, parsed fail-closed.
- **INV-11** (IP auto-detect mode): the box's setting `{auto, interval_min}`, default `auto=true`, `interval_min=60`, interval in **1…1440** minutes (1 minute to 24 hours, for example 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 minutes, 1, 6, 12, 24 hours; outside the range → 400 `bad_interval`); only `admin` changes it (`PUT /exposure/box-ip/settings`); changing the interval or turning it off does not delete the IP list. The job `exposure/auto-detect` (one tick per minute, starting up to half a tick early) runs `Detect` when `auto` is on and (it has never run, that is the first time on install or when the module has just been turned on, or `last_run.at + interval_min` has been reached); turning `auto` on from off runs `Detect` at once within that PUT (unless it probed within the last 5 minutes, so that constant toggling does not become a way to probe in bursts). A scheduled probe is *light* (1 round, 3 requests); a *full* probe (5 rounds) runs the first time, at most once an hour, and at every press of "Detect now" (polite to other people's free services, avoiding being blocked). The result of the last run (`last_run {at, ok, message}`) is in `GET /exposure/targets` → `box_ip`. `auto=false`: nothing runs by itself, only "Detect now" (`POST /exposure/box-ip/verify`). A user deletes an IP (`DELETE /exposure/targets/{id}`, INV-7); if the box still uses that IP to reach the Internet and `auto` is still on, the next probe adds the IP again as a new target (no commitment, no snapshot). The job writes `core_audit` with the actor `system` **only when something changes**: a new IP is added, an expired IP is seen again, a change between working and failing (the first time counts as a change), an address that was refused and the previous run did not say so; a normal run (the same IP, still fine) only updates `last_run`. A person's press is always audited. A stored time that is more than 5 minutes in the future (the clock once ran fast) is treated as `now`: validity is no more than 30 days and the probe is due at once. `TestContract_INV11_AutoDetectSchedule`, `TestContract_INV11_ManualModeAndSettings`, `TestContract_INV11_RemovedIPComesBack`, `TestContract_INV11_LightAndFullChecks`, `TestContract_INV11_AuditOnlyChanges`, `TestContract_INV11_RefusedAddressIsAuditedOnce`, `TestContract_INV4_ClockThatWasAhead`
- **INV-12**: The scan gate for `scan`: `Service.CheckScannable` is the only way `scan` can ask whether an external target can be scanned (PRE-3). `POST …/scan` of exposure checks INV-1 (and INV-13) and then hands the scanning to the `Runner` of the `scan` module (running in the background after returning 202, INV-18) (`cmd/vedette` `scanRunner`, package `modules/scan/surface`, `scan` INV-12), set through `Config.Runner` when the service is built; `ScanTarget.User` is the person who pressed. **Scope (LEGAL §3)**: the runner only port-scans an address when `Service.ScannableIP(ctx, actor, ip)` returns `true`: the address is an IP target of exposure and passes exactly the gate of a scan of it (INV-1, INV-13; it may run a full probe as in INV-13, the actor being the person who pressed); an address that is not public (`IsPublicIPv4`), not an IP target, not committed, expired or paused → `false`, with no error. The names of a domain (the CT log) are only listed and checked for a web page by name on port 80 and 443. **One run at a time on the box**: from INV-36, `POST …/scan` of a different target while a run is going (or a run is waiting) goes into the queue (202 `queued`); a second run of the very target that is being scanned, a synchronous `ScanNow`/`Scan` and OpenVAS again while a run is going or waiting (until the previous run ends, even after a 202) → a synchronous 409 `busy` ("Another external scan is running on this box. Wait for it to finish, then try again."). **A target at most once per `ScanEvery` (10 minutes)**, counted from when the runner was called (kept in memory, forgotten on restart) → 429 `rate_limited` ("<value> was scanned at <time>. Scan it again after <time>: at most one scan of a target every 10 minutes."); a runner refusal because the module is off does not count. The runner is code of `scan` but follows the **switch of `exposure`** (the v1 package turns on `exposure` and leaves `scan`, the LAN nuclei scan in Preview, off): it only needs `exposure` on, with `scan` off it still scans; `exposure` off → 409 `module_off` (`ErrScanOff`, "The External exposure module is off, so the box cannot scan. An admin can turn it on in Settings, Modules.") after the gate, with no probe; turned off during a run, the run stops (`status: stopped`). The probe log is still the `scan_probe` table of `scan` (the migration runs for every module, even when off); service identification and nuclei templates (INV-19, INV-23) go through the `scan` container according to the `sensor-active` switch. A runner error ends the run with `status: failed` with `error {code, message}` exactly the code and sentence below (the user reads it on the page through `GET /exposure/scan`) and **writes no row, emits no event**: 502 `unreachable` when the CT search (`crt.sh`) does not answer (`ErrCTDown`: a run missing subdomains would make their rows look as if they had disappeared) or when the scan exceeds 5 minutes (`ErrScanTooLong`); 400 `too_many` when the CT log has more than 300 names under the domain (`TooManyNames`: no partial scan, because whichever part is kept would change by the day); another error is `failed` with `internal` (logged). With no `Runner` (a build without the `scan` module; a hand-built test) → 501 `not_implemented` after the gate. **Every attempt has an `exposure.scan` row in `core_audit`** (the person who pressed, the target, the result): success `"<n> subdomains, <m> ports"` in the same transaction as the snapshot; not successful (gate refused, busy, too early, module off, error) `"not done: <code>: <message>"` or `"failed: …"`, written even when the caller has disconnected. A finished scan → the run's rows (INV-37) and `exposure.scan.written` (POST-1; `cmd/vedette/exposureports.go` turns it into `exposure.port.opened` for the `new_port` trigger of `response`). `TestContract_INV12_ScanGate`, `TestContract_INV12_OneScanAtATimeAndNotTooOften`, `TestContract_INV12_ScannableIP`, `TestHandlers_ScanWithoutRunnerIs501`, `cmd/vedette` `TestExposureRunnerFollowsTheExposureModule`, `TestExposureRunnerResult`, `TestExposureScanNeverProbesNonPublicAddresses`
- **INV-13** (a check at scan time): an IP stays in the list for 30 days from the last time seen (INV-4), but a dynamic IP (a modem that changes IP on restart) may have been given to someone else within those 30 days, so before each scan of an IP (`CheckScannable`, `POST …/scan`) it is checked against the network, like rechecking a domain's TXT (INV-1c): it may be scanned when the latest probe, or the latest *full* probe, ran within the past 5 minutes (`CurrentFor`) and saw that IP. If not, it runs **one** full probe at once (5 rounds, the union of IPs, so a load-balanced egress still passes) with the caller as the actor, at most once per 5 minutes: within that window the result of the latest full probe is reused, including a result of "not seen". This run does **not** count toward the user's 10/hour (INV-5) and shares `detectMu` with every probe. Not seen, or the probe cannot conclude (no service answers, the services disagree): **refused** (fail closed) with 409 `ip_not_current` ("This box no longer reaches the Internet through <ip>; it was last seen <time>. Scans of it are paused until the box uses it again."), `CheckScannable` returns `(false, BlockedNotCurrent)`; the IP stays in the list and can be scanned again as soon as the box uses that IP again. A target that fails another condition (no commitment, expired…) is refused for that reason, with no network probe. **The state lives on the target** (QA 2026-11-09): `GET /exposure/targets` and `GET /exposure/targets/{id}` set `blocked_reason: ip_not_current` and `scannable: false` for an IP that satisfies the other conditions when **the latest full probe that reached a conclusion** (the services agreed; the column `exp_setting.last_full_ok_at`) did not see that IP and no later probe saw it (a sighting renews `verified_at` to the time of the run, so `verified_at < last_full_ok_at` is enough; the function `checks.paused`). The state rests on stored facts, **with no time window**: it stays until a later probe sees the IP (light or full). A light probe (1 round, which can miss a load-balanced IP) never sets this state, it can only clear it when it sees the IP; a failed probe (no service answers, no agreement) changes nothing (`last_full_ok_at` does not move). No full probe with a conclusion yet = nothing is known against the IP, no block. The scan gate is unchanged (the 5-minute window above, a new full probe when it is past), so an IP that is "Paused" still has Scan now locked in the GUI; "Verify again" at step 1 is the recheck action (no new button added). Reading does not probe the network. The GUI shows the "Scans paused" card, a Scan now button locked with the reason and the "Paused" status in the list. Dates and times in every API message have one form: "9 Nov 2026" and "9 Nov 2026, 17:00" in the box's time zone (`Config.Location`), not ISO, not "UTC". `TestContract_INV13_IPThatIsNoLongerUsedIsNotScanned`, `TestContract_INV13_LoadBalancedEgressStaysScannable`, `TestContract_INV13_FailedProbeRefuses`, `TestContract_INV13_NoProbeBeforeTheOtherConditions`, `TestContract_INV13_PausedIsAStateOfTheTarget`, `TestContract_INV13_FullCheckWithoutTheIPPausesItAndItStays`, `TestContract_INV13_LightCheckNeverPausesButCanClear`, `TestContract_INV13_LaterFullCheckThatSeesItClears`, `TestContract_INV13_FailedCheckChangesNothing`, `TestContract_INV13_DomainIsNeverPaused`, `TestContract_INV13_PausedAndRetryOverHTTP`, `TestContract_INV13_MessagesUseOneHumanDateFormat`
- **INV-14** (the ignore list): removing an IP (`DELETE /exposure/targets/{id}?ignore=true`, admin) puts it on the ignore list `exp_ignored {value, ignored_by, ignored_at}` in the same transaction as the delete; only an IP can be ignored (a domain → 400 `bad_value`), at most 200 IPs (`MaxIgnored`, 400 `too_many`). An ignored IP is **never** added again by `Detect` (automatic or pressed; a probe that only sees an ignored IP is a normal result: `last_run.ok=true`, `items` empty, the message says the IP is in Ignored) and because it is not a target it is never scanned. The list **only blocks, never allows** (FORBID-2): `Restore` (`DELETE /exposure/box-ip/ignored/{ip}`, admin; 400 `bad_value` if not a canonical IPv4, 404 `not_found` if not in the list) only deletes the row, so the next probe can add that IP again as a new target with no commitment; it adds nothing and approves nothing. ## Preconditions / Postconditions
- **INV-35**: **A public IP typed by the user** (`ipproof.go`, `POST /exposure/targets` with `kind: "ip"`; an IP target still has two sources: `origin: "box"` is an IP the box probed itself, INV-2, and `origin: "person"` is a typed-in IP). A typed-in IP goes into the list at once (a canonical public IPv4, INV-3; IPv6, private, reserved, CGNAT: 400 `ip_not_allowed`; an IP that is in the Ignored list: 400 `bad_value`), and is **never scanned, and never sent to Shodan, without evidence found at that moment**: (a) a token file `/.well-known/vedette-verify.txt` served on the IP itself, http or https (the ACME HTTP-01 model, RFC 8555 §8.3): the file body is the target's own token (16 hex, column `ip_token`, random), connected to exactly the IP by a fixed dialer (no proxy, a redirect only to that very IP, a body of at most 512 bytes, 5 seconds per request, the certificate is not verified because the token is the evidence); (b) forward-confirmed reverse DNS (RFC 8601 §2.7.3): the IP's PTR is a name under a domain that the box can prove at that moment (the same `prove` rule, the domain's own evidence on itself covering the names under it) and that name resolves back to the IP. An A record of a proven domain pointing at the IP is **not** evidence. The evidence keeps no state, like a domain's: the scan gate (`gate`) and `CheckOutside` look it up again right before using it (`liveTypedProof`), writing `last_check`, and one pass renews; not found means 409 `proof_missing` (`blocked_reason: proof_missing`). It expires after `ValidDays` (30) days from the last pass (`verified_at`, `expires_at` in the API): expired means 409 `expired` even if the file is still there, and the user proves it again. The `ip-proof` job (every hour) rechecks by itself `IPRecheckLeft` (7 days) before the deadline and then retries every `IPRecheckRetry` (6 hours) until it passes or expires (`next_check_at` in the API). The ownership check is once every 30 seconds per target and within the box's shared bucket (`domainCheckAllowed`, like a domain). A typed-in IP that the box probes and finds to be its own Internet egress IP becomes the box's IP (`renewIP`: `typed = 0`). Each list row has `kind`, `origin`, the evidence deadline, `scan_state` (`unproved`, `expired`, `paused`, `needs_terms`, `ready`, `scanning`), the latest result and `open_exposures` (the number of open ports of the latest scan); an IP the box probed itself that has not accepted the terms gets `scan_state: needs_terms`. The results, open ports and Shodan view of an existing target are per target (`/exposure/tree/{id}/…`, `/outside`). Port and page scanning in place (the runner of scan) and OpenVAS trust exposure's gate: they use the live result (at most 5 minutes, a failure 30 seconds, not beyond the evidence deadline, one result per target, shared between the gate, ScannableIP, the steps and the terms of a scan), OpenVAS looks up the evidence again before starting and refuses when it is gone. The terms sentence of a typed-in IP is "I own {ip} or am authorised to have it tested", the shared-address warning is always shown, and the disruptive tick only when the user ticks it separately. With the terms accepted, the deep steps (service identification, nuclei templates, ZAP, OpenVAS) go to the executor with `ScanTarget.Domain.Typed`: the gate's live evidence (the token file or PTR + the covering domain), the box-bound token of the IP taken from the host (`DomainFence.Token`, the IP listed like a domain), the executor checks again by the same rule (`core/domainfence` typed.go) and refuses fake, missing or expired evidence; an old host/worker refuses with the sentence "Update the box". With no terms, no domain fence or an old token: the step says the reason (`ScanTarget.TypedWhy`) instead of reaching the box-IP-only fence. Test: `ipproof_test.go`.
- **PRE-1** (`StartScan(actor, targetID, noticeVersion)`, `ScanNow` waits for the result): `operator` and INV-1; the first time for a target with no commitment only an admin with two-step (INV-17). **POST-1**: (when the run ends `done`, INV-18) the run's rows (`exp_host`, `exp_address`, `exp_service`, `exp_issue`, `exp_scan`; INV-37); emits `exposure.scan.written`.

## Forbidden
- **FORBID-1**: Scanning a target whose ownership is not authenticated or has expired.
- **FORBID-2**: Accepting a public IP typed by the user. The customer can only turn auto-detect on/off, change the interval and delete an IP; not add an IP.
- **FORBID-3**: A consent text (removed) and a separate tick box (removed); the commitment is only recorded by the first Scan now of an admin with two-step.
- **FORBID-6**: Recording a commitment with a sentence that the person pressing has not seen (a version mismatch) or by someone who is not an admin with two-step.
- **FORBID-4**: Depending on `inventory`'s commitment (`scope_confirmed`) for an external target; the two commitments are independent.
- **FORBID-5**: Sending internal data (the device store, logs) out to the CT and "my IP" services.
- **FORBID-7**: Sending Shodan anything other than one IP of the box itself that passed the INV-21 gate, or sending without an admin's press (a job, background asking, remembered consent).
- **FORBID-8**: Running a nuclei template on anything that is not an identified port of the box's own authenticated IP (or of an address that passes the INV-28 fence) with a matching exploited CVE; sending a probe before its probe log row is written; saying "not vulnerable"/"safe" when a template does not match (INV-23).
- **FORBID-10**: Letting Greenbone scan anything other than the box's own authenticated, committed, non-paused IP (a domain, another IP; the only exception: an address that passes the INV-28 fence, with `GreenboneFence` when it is not the box's IP), starting a task before its probe log row is written, or reading an invalid GMP report or "Greenbone found nothing" as "not affected"; letting a `greenbone` row replace, close or hide a `version`/`product`/`template` row (INV-25).
- **FORBID-9**: Sending ZAP a request that is not a GET, or to anything that is not the web page of a target that passed the gate of that run (forms, the AJAX spider, an active scan, names of another domain, private addresses), or reading an invalid ZAP report as "no alerts" (INV-24).
- **FORBID-11**: Sending any deep step (port scan, -sV, nuclei, ZAP at the terms' depth, OpenVAS) to an address of a name under a domain when the domain's terms have not been accepted, when `deepGate` has not just rechecked, to a private address or IPv6, or to an address that is not the box's without a `ScanTarget.Domain` carrying the token of `DomainFence` (INV-28).
