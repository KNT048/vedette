# Exposure: change impact map

What else to check when you change part of this module. Signatures and behaviour are in the code; this file names the readers.
Any change to what is sent, to where, or how often also changes `PRIVACY.md` flows 9 and 15 and the Settings → Privacy screen,
in the same commit.

## Switching the module off

Routes, detection and scans stop; tables stay. The scan module gets no external targets and response gets no new-port events.
