| D-416 | **The audit log is kept by time, not by a small row count; automatic lines stay but can be left out; devices and findings are shown by name** (supervisor, 2026-11-13, customer walk 1 W1-34; S-CORE-3, core INV-17, INV-37). (1) **Retention**: `core_audit` keeps the lines of the last **365 days, at most 200,000 lines**; the oldest run goes first. Before this, the screen said "The box keeps the latest 1000 lines" but the box kept everything forever (the 1,000 was the preview's browser-storage cap, `mocks/stores.ts`), so the trail had no bound on a real box and the screen told the customer something untrue. Core job `core/audit-prune` (every 6 h; `audit.Log.Prune`) deletes only a contiguous prefix, never the newest line, moves `core_chain_anchor` and lowers `core_chain_head` in the same transaction (`chainhead.Prune`), and writes one `audit.pruned` line by `system` ("N lines: older than 365 days (before …) or beyond the newest 200000"); the chain verifies after every prune. The append-only trigger stays: migration `0011` lets a delete through only for lines at or before the line the anchor names, so the prune must move the anchor first and nothing else can delete (the purge still drops the triggers for its wipe). 200,000 lines is about 80 MB at the size of today's lines and covers a year at the walk's day-one rate (≈330 lines/day) many times over; past it the oldest go even if younger than 365 days. Not configurable (ponytail: nobody asked; the AI log's own `retention_days` is a different log). (2) **Automatic lines stay in the audit log**: "Finding created" and the other lines by `system`/`system:<part>` record what the box changed by itself and are kept (GitHub's audit log also records automated events such as Dependabot alerts); they are no longer what pushes people's lines out, because retention is by time. They can be hidden: `GET /core/audit` and `/core/audit/export` take `actor=people`, which leaves out `system` and `system:<part>` (a person, the console and an MCP token stay); the Who filter offers "Not the system (people and tools)". The default view still shows every line (nothing hidden by surprise). (3) **Names**: `GET /core/audit` returns `names` (id → display name) for the device (`a-…`) and finding (`f-…`) ids in the page's targets and details, through an optional `core.AuditNamer` that `cmd/vedette` implements over inventory (`DeviceRefs`: host name, else IP, else what the box knows) and findings (`Get`: "<CVE> on <device>"); core imports neither module. The page shows the name with the stored text in small type below; an id the box no longer has stays as it is. The CSV export keeps the stored text (it is the record).
