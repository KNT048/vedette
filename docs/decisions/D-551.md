| D-551 | **Incidents: "Not sent" says why, from the decision notify made, with a link to fix it; operators acknowledge or resolve many incidents at once (beta item 16 / A12; S-ALERT-3; notify INV-24, INV-25).** (1) **Reason = the delivery decision, kept.** `enqueue` already decides per channel (`Decide`: min severity INV-4, quiet hours INV-5); for an incident it now keeps the outcome on `notify_incident.unsent` (+ `unsent_floor`, migration 0013) in the same transaction: `queued`, `quiet_hours`, `no_channel`, `channels_off`, `below_min_severity`; the queue overwrites it when it drops a held message (`channels_off`, `below_min_severity`, `dropped_stale`). At read time a message still in the queue wins (`queued`, or `quiet_hours` with the release time), then failed attempts (`failed`, the channel and INV-6's friendly error), then the kept decision. Recorded rather than recomputed because the channels change: an incident from before a channel was added must not claim the channel "would" have taken it. Incidents opened by an older binary (NULL) read today's channels and say `unknown` when today's channels would take it, instead of guessing. (2) **Words + link**: one plain English sentence from the box (`text`), the GUI adds one fix link per reason to Settings → Alert channels (admins only: the page is admin's). (3) **Not built**: `rule_off` (notify has no per-kind mute rule) and `repeat` (a repeat folds into the open incident, INV-10, it is never its own row); `first_run_summary` comes from D-550's own rule (INV-23: a low/medium bad-connection or log incident held in the first 36 hours, column `first_run`), wired in when the two branches meet. (4) **Bulk**: `POST /notify/incidents/status {ids 1..500, acknowledged|resolved, note?}`, the shape and wording of the findings bulk change (D-484): all or nothing, duplicates once, a resolved incident keeps its state and note, acknowledging an acknowledged one changes nothing, every changed incident gets PATCH's fields and its own audit row; no bulk reopen; selection only of rows shown (a filter never acts on hidden rows). **Top-tier:** PagerDuty shows per-incident notification log with the reason a notification was suppressed or not sent (urgency/notification rules, maintenance windows, "Suppressed" alerts) and lets responders select incidents and Acknowledge/Resolve in bulk (https://support.pagerduty.com/main/docs/incidents , https://support.pagerduty.com/main/docs/notification-content-and-behavior); Grafana Alerting's notification history and contact-point "last delivery" show failed deliveries with the error and a Test button (https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/alerting/monitor-status/view-alert-state/ , https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/alerting/configure-notifications/manage-contact-points/); Microsoft Defender XDR / Sentinel incident queues select many incidents and change status with one comment, each incident keeping its own activity log (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-xdr/manage-incidents , https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/sentinel/investigate-incidents); Datadog monitors show "muted"/"downtime" as the reason a notification did not go (https://docs.datadoghq.com/monitors/downtimes/). **Not chosen:** computing the reason only from today's channels (lies about past incidents once a channel is added); a separate "not sent" history table (one row per incident is enough, ponytail); bulk reopen (reopening checks the dedupe key one by one, 409 `already_open`); resolve-overwrites-note (Defender and the findings bulk keep what someone wrote). | 2026-11-13 |
