| D-497 | **A device deleted in Devices leaves the open new-device incidents (W6-3, S-ALERT; Trưởng's known limit).** The first-discovery summary "N devices found on your network" kept listing (and counting) a device after it was deleted, and the device's own "A new unknown device appeared" incident stayed open with nothing left to approve. notify now listens to `inventory.asset.deleted {asset_id}`: an open summary drops the device from its list and count (resolved when none is left), the device's own open incident is resolved by `system` with the reason in its note and one audit row; no message is sent. Resolved incidents and other kinds (trap hits, findings) keep the device: they are a record of what happened. Sources: Microsoft Defender XDR resolves an alert automatically when its entity is removed or the condition no longer applies ("resolved by system") and keeps the alert history (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-xdr/manage-incidents), UniFi and Fing drop a forgotten device from the client lists but keep past events. | 2026-11-13 |
