# features/reports

Story: S-REP-1 (one-page weekly report: preview, send schedule, recipients, send now, history, print/save PDF), S-REP-2 (safety assessment under the Personal Data Protection Law: organisation form, items generated from the inventory and findings, the disclaimer "an aid to assessment, not legal advice", print/save PDF).
Provides and consumes no slots. Periodic report: three tabs Report / History / Settings; a sent report opens at `/reports/sent/:id` exactly as it was sent; printing uses `window.print()` with `.print-report` (index.css, A4, colours kept). Assessment print page: `/reports/print/pdpl`. No PDF library.
Tables: `rep_definition` (the one report: options, recipients; its schedule is the shared `reports.def:<id>`), `rep_history` (each sent document, kept exactly as sent; the mock calls it `rep_sent`), `rep_pdpl`. The mock document is `document.ts`; `conformance.test.ts` checks the contract. API contract: `contract.ts` → `types.ts`.
AI commentary shows only inside `AiBlock` and records `recordAiRun` (it does not go through the ai module token cap, because the cap lives in that module's mock).
The legal content was checked on 2026-11-06 against Law 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP (Official Gazette no. 18, 18/01/2026): the Decree article numbers were read straight from the Gazette; the Law article numbers through the full text in the legal database. The organisation size decides Articles 21, 22 and Article 33(2) via Article 41 of Decree 356 (`regimeOf` in `pdpl.ts`). The information-system safety level (1-5) is a separate regulation, not part of the data protection law.
