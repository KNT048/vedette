| D-560 | **A port that the previous scan saw and the newest scan did not is "Not seen in the last scan" and still counts as open; it is "closed" only when a second scan also misses it (beta item 24; exposure INV-22, S-EXP-1).** Before: one scan that missed a port (packet loss, a rate limit, a host that answered slowly) made the diff say the port was gone, a false "fixed". Now `saveScan` carries the port (and the service read on it) into the new snapshot with `last_seen` = the day it was last seen; the next scan on another day either sees it (normal row, no "new port" alert, because the row exists on both days) or drops it (closed; the compare says "closed: not seen in two scans, <date> and <date>"). The runner cannot forge `last_seen` (`sanitize` drops it). Chosen over an immediate re-probe of the missing ports because the runner belongs to the scan module and a re-probe needs a runner change: written down as a deferred line. Two consecutive misses (two scan days) follows the pattern of attack-surface products, which keep a "last seen" time on a service instead of deleting it after one miss (Censys, runZero, Shodan expose last-seen/last-updated fields).
