| D-487 | **Repeated failed logins raise an incident from day 1 (S-LOG-1, walk finding W1-22).** A customer sent 3 × "Failed password for invalid user admin from 45.155.205.233" and nothing was raised: the signature path never repeats one signature (every account name is its own), and the anomaly job waits for a 7-day baseline and a 24 h warm-up. New rule in `modules/logs/authfail.go`, fed by the lines the counters already read (the remote address of D-210, so never the log sender or the box): **5 failed attempts from one remote address within 10 minutes on one source = one `logs.anomaly.detected` event** (target `auth:<address>`, notify opens a `log_anomaly` incident), naming the source, address, count, the account names tried (at most 5, plain text, 32 characters each) and what to do. Lines read: sshd "Failed password for", "Invalid user", PAM "authentication failure"; Windows 4625 (and 4624 as the success). sshd writes up to three lines per attempt, so an attempt is the largest of the three counts, not their sum. Dedupe: one incident per (source, address) per hour; a login "Accepted …"/4624 from the same address after failures raises severity to high (the first incident when the acceptance came before the threshold, else a second incident `auth:<address> accepted`). A LAN address counts and the text says "on your own network". Bounds: 2048 pairs followed (new pairs ignored while full), 32 timestamps per class, 20 incidents an hour, state in memory (a restart forgets at most 10 minutes). Thresholds: fail2ban's default is maxretry 5 / findtime 10m (the number a Linux admin already expects); Wazuh rules 5710/5712 ("sshd brute force") fire at frequency 8 in 120 s, a tighter window for an active attack; CrowdSec's `ssh-bf` scenario leaks one failure per 10 s with capacity 5 (about 5 in under a minute, slower attackers drain); Elastic's prebuilt "Potential SSH Brute Force Detected" and Graylog's authentication-failure-count alerts use a count per source address over minutes. We take 5 in 10 minutes: low enough that a customer testing with 5-6 wrong logins sees it, and wide enough to catch a slow guesser that a 2-minute window misses; a customer's own typos (1 to 3) stay quiet. Hardening after review: a line counts only when the syslog program is sshd/sshd-session (PAM "authentication failure" also from login, su, sudo) and the phrase starts the message, or by a shipped Windows event id; the words inside a web log or an account name never count. The incident that says a login was accepted is never cut by the 20-an-hour budget (medium ones are, and a cut one opens again at its next failure); a table that is full evicts the oldest pair that never opened an incident; such pairs go after 10 minutes; IPv6 counts per /64 (sshguard does the same); any incident with an accepted login has the target `auth:<address> accepted`. Not done: a login that succeeds on the 5th try after 4 failures raises nothing (the threshold rule's limit: the incident opens at the 5th failure, and an acceptance only escalates after it); correlation is within one log source only; account names are plain text but some clients may render a name as a link; Windows only counts when the profile finds the address (Source Network Address); FortiGate admin-login failures are not read (their lines carry no "from" the same way); no blocking (Vedette is not inline).
