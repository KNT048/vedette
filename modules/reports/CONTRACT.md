# Reports Contract

> Tier: optional (off by default) · Story: S-REP-1, S-REP-2 · Last verified: 2026-11-23 (every sentence checked against the code)
> Source of the actual behaviour: `web/src/features/reports/{contract,types,mocks,document,pdpl}.ts`. Legal content checked 2026-11-06: Law 91/2025/QH15, Decree 356/2025/ND-CP.

## Invariants (always true)
- **INV-1** The periodic report is **made when it is opened** (`GET /reports/document`) from the shared read models, one read per section: findings through `findings.View` and their state events (`fnd_finding_event`), external exposure through the exposure rows, detections through the incidents of `notify`, coverage through the inventory, the module registry and the feeds' own status. No figure is generated by AI. A device a person ignored, and its findings, are in no section (`findings.Views` leaves them out, as every findings list does). `TestDocumentGolden` (the whole document of a fixture network and its email, `testdata/document_week.golden.json`).
- **INV-2** A sent report keeps **the document exactly as it was sent** (`rep_history.document`); reopening it never recomputes. `TestSendNowKeepsTheDocumentAsSent`.
- **INV-3** One definition (`rep_definition`, id `security`): one template with a switch per section, a period (`last_7_days`, `last_30_days`, `calendar_month` = the last whole month in the organisation's zone), a grouping of Still open (`device` or `cve`), "include devices not added yet" (on by default), "include not relevant" (off by default), charts, at most 20 recipients, each a valid address of at most 120 characters (lower-cased, without duplicates). Each refusal names the field (`checkDefinition`). The definition nobody saved is the default and sends nothing.
- **INV-4** Email is sent only through the `notify` email channel (the customer's SMTP, PRIVACY flow 5); the module has no SMTP of its own. The mail is a plain-text digest (`digest`) linking to `/reports/sent/<id>`; the server builds no HTML or PDF.
- **INV-9** **Handled this period** is read from the findings' state events, not from the current row: the last handling inside the period with who (`by`, `system` = Vedette) and how (`resolution`, `via`, `from_version` → `to_version`, `note`, `accepted_until`), from the event's stored fields. A person's note on Vedette's patch credit keeps the credit and adds the note. A finding reopened and handled again in the period is listed once with its last handling. "Patched by Vedette: version moved 6.24 → 7.23 (9 Nov)" is `HandledLine`, the words of the pages. `TestDocumentWinRARIsPatchedByVedette`, `TestHistoryOfAFindingHandledTwice`; on real modules `cmd/vedette` `TestWiringReportsDocumentFromRealModules`.
- **INV-10** The summary counts what the sections list: `open_now` = Still open's total, `new` = New's total, `handled` = Handled's total; `open_before` is the findings open at the start of the period by their events. With "include not relevant" off, a finding handled as not relevant in the period counts nowhere (it was never a real flaw). With "include devices not added yet" off, their findings, their coverage count and their device on a detection are left out. `TestDocumentSummaryAgreesWithTheSections`, `TestDocumentOptions`.
- **INV-11** Bounded size: each list of a document holds at most 100 rows (`MaxRows`; `more` says how many were left out, the counts are of everything), the Detections section names at most 5 incidents, the send history keeps 52 entries. Strings from the network are cleaned (control and bidirectional characters, one line, a length limit) before they enter a document.
- **INV-12** Every mutation (definition, send, organisation information, assessment, AI commentary) writes `core_audit` in the same write (SYS-3). The print pages and the sent page are SPA routes; no PDF is made on the server.
- **INV-13** The send schedule is a `core/schedule` owner keyed `reports.def:<id>` (`ScheduleOwner`): weekly or monthly, any time, outside the scan window, off until an administrator turns it on, Monday 08:00 in the organisation's zone by default. When it comes due its `Start` marks the definition (`send_due_at`, and `send_by` for a person's "Run once now") inside the schedule's transaction, or skips with the reason (no recipient, email channel not ready); the `report-send` job clears the mark before it sends (each due time is sent at most once) and drops a due time more than 24 hours late. `TestScheduleStartAndTheSendJob`, `cmd/vedette` `TestTheBoxRegistersTheScheduleOwners`.
- **INV-5** The AI commentary (`AiSummary`) is produced only by the `ai` module (`pdpl-assessment`), always shown in an "AI generated" block, and **does not change the figures**; it obeys the internal-data rules because the report contains device data. Without AI → the report is still complete, `ai_available=false`.
- **INV-6** PDPL report: the obligation regime (`Obligation.regime`: required/optional/exempt) for `dpia`, `dpo` is **computed deterministically** from the organisation's size (`size`, per Article 38 of Law 91/2025/QH15 and Article 41 of Decree 356/2025/ND-CP), not decided by AI; the other obligations are always required. The information-system level (`level` 1..5 or empty) is a separate regime, not part of the Personal Data Protection Law.
- **INV-7** Every PDPL report carries the disclaimer "supports assessment, is not legal advice" and states the legal sources; this sentence cannot be removed (`LEGAL.md`).
- **INV-8** `PdplOrg` is checked field by field when saved (`PUT /reports/pdpl/org`); `measures` (firewall, patching, eol, unapproved, remote, logging, honeypot, scan, backup) carry `status ∈ {ok, attention, unknown}` — missing data because a module is off is `unknown`, **never `ok`**; `coverage[]` states which modules are on.

## Preconditions (before calling)
- **PRE-1** `PUT /reports/definition`: admin role. `POST /reports/send`: operator role, with at least one recipient (400 `no_recipients`) and the `notify` email channel ready (409 `email_not_ready`); otherwise nothing is sent and no history is written.
- **PRE-2** `POST /reports/pdpl/ai-summary`: the `ai` module is on and the task has an allowed profile (409 `ai_unavailable`).
- **PRE-3** `POST /reports/pdpl/generate`: valid organisation information has been saved (409 `org_required`). `POST /reports/pdpl/ai-summary` needs a generated report (409 `report_required`); if the report is regenerated while the AI is writing, 409 `report_changed` and the commentary is not saved.

## Postconditions (after completion)
- **POST-1** After a send attempt with a recipient: one `rep_history` entry (`sent` or `failed` with a fixed message; a document that could not be made is kept as its header), and the email goes to exactly the recipient list at the time of sending.
- **POST-2** A due schedule sends once, with `trigger=schedule` (a person's "Run once now": `manual` under their name).
- **POST-3** PDPL `generate`: produces a `PdplReport` from the devices, findings and module state; it has `generated_at`.
- **POST-4** The AI commentary of the assessment is stored in `rep_pdpl`, and one `ai_activity` entry is written through the `ai` module.

## Forbidden (never)
- **FORBID-1** AI changing figures or legal conclusions; AI commentary without the "AI generated" label.
- **FORBID-2** Sending a report to an address that is not in the saved `recipients`.
- **FORBID-3** Stating "compliant/meets the standard" while `measures` still has `unknown` or `attention`.
- **FORBID-4** Reading another module's tables directly (use the ports, IMPACT).
- **FORBID-5** An external PDF library/service or resources from a CDN in the print page (legal.md).

## Tables owned
`rep_definition`, `rep_history`, `rep_pdpl`.

## Events
- **Emits**: no bus events; every change and every send writes `core_audit` (`report.definition`, `report.send`, `report.pdpl_org`, `report.pdpl_generate`, `report.ai_summary`).
- **Receives**: the job `report-send` (every 20 seconds) from `core/jobs`, and the due times of its `core/schedule` owner.

## When the module is off (SYS-5)
No `/reports/*` routes, no send job; its schedule skips with the module-off reason. `rep_*` are kept.

## Ports (cmd/vedette/reports.go)
`Inventory.Devices` (watched devices, the box included; `Agent` = an agent reports its software), `FindingViews.Views` (`findings.Service.Views` mapped through `View.JSON`, with the state events) and `.Reasons` (`findings.Service.View`, only for the open findings of layers 1 to 4 the document lists), `Exposure.Targets` (exposure `Tree`, `Services`, `Issues`: open services, those closed in the period, open issues by engine and severity), `Detections.Period` (`notify.Service.IncidentsIn`), `Feeds.Health` (the feeds module's own `GET /feeds/sources` and `GET /feeds/status` answers, `feeds.IsStale`), `Findings.All` and `KEV.Entries` (the assessment), `Mailer.Ready/Send` (notify), `AI` (ai: `pdpl-assessment`), `Modules.Enabled`. Reports does not read other modules' tables.

## Dependencies
- **DEPENDS ON**: `core` (jobs, schedules, audit, the organisation's zone), `inventory`, `findings`, `feeds`; soft: `notify` (sending, incidents), `exposure`, `ai` (assessment commentary), the detection modules (`honeypot`, `logs`, `sensor-passive`, `ids`).
- **DEPENDED ON BY**: no module reads `reports` data; `core/server/modules.go` routes `/reports/*` to this module.
