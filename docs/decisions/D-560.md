| D-560 | **A port that the last observation had open and a scan that checked its address did not report is "Missed by the last scan" and still counts as open; it is "closed" only when a second scan in a row also misses it (beta item 24; exposure INV-22, S-EXP-1).** Before: one scan that missed a port (packet loss, a rate limit, a slow host) made the diff say the port was gone, a false "fixed". Now `saveScan` keeps the port (and the service read on it, so its exploited CVEs stay) in the new snapshot with `last_seen` and `misses`. Misses are counted per scan, not per day, against the newest snapshot including today's: a customer who fixed a port and presses Scan now twice sees it closed; one flaky scan never closes it; a sighting earlier today is not forgotten by one miss; seen again resets the count and sends no "new port" alert (the row exists on both days). A scan that did not check the address (a domain scan port-scans only names' IPs that `ScannableIP` allows; lapsed, paused, deleted or skipped IPs are not) is not a miss: the row stays as it was and says "Not checked since <date>"; an address leaves with its host only when every name that pointed at it is in this scan with another address; a name that failed to resolve this time, or an address no name pointed at, is not checked, so its rows are kept (`unchecked`, whatever their miss count). The runner cannot forge the mark (`sanitize` drops it). Time bound: Censys marks a service pending eviction after its first failed scan and removes it 72 hours later (Durumeric et al., "Censys: A Map of Internet Hosts and Services", 2025), a time rule. Vedette has no scheduled exposure scan (scans happen on Scan now), so a time rule would close ports nobody re-checked; the minimal honest version is: always show the last-seen date, label rows no scan checked "Not checked since <date>", and never claim a closure without two covering scans. Ceiling: a kept row counts as open with no expiry; upgrade: stop counting it after 72 hours without a covering scan. Wording does not promise a scan: "Vedette says it is closed only after a second scan also misses it; use Scan now to check again".
