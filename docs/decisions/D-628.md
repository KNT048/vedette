| D-628 | **A domain's ownership proof covers its subdomains, may sit at `_vedette-verify.<name>`, and never crosses a public suffix (exposure, J6, kien G10; amends D-229 and exposure INV-1 (c), INV-6).** (1) A TXT record `vedette-verify=<token>` on a name proves that name and every name under it, at any depth: a verified beyondnetglobal.com covers git.beyondnetglobal.com with no second record. The check of a target (Check, and again right before every scan, INV-1 (c)) looks at the name and each parent down to its registered domain (at most 8 names), and at `_vedette-verify.` before each of them. (2) `_vedette-verify.<name>` takes the record for a name that is a CNAME, where DNS allows no other record (the provider answers "Record could not be added"). The executors' domain fence (core/domainfence) accepts it there too. (3) A proof never sits on or crosses a public suffix of the Public Suffix List (golang.org/x/net/publicsuffix, already linked), and **both** of its sections count. ICANN's suffixes (com, co.uk) are one boundary. The private ones (ddns.net, github.io, herokuapp.com) are the other: many customers register under them, so with the ICANN section alone a record on ddns.net would cover every customer's name under it. A public suffix is never a target (400 `bad_value`). (4) The token that counts at a name is the token of any of this box's domain targets at or under that name. Every token is the box's own (random, or bound to the box by the domain fence), so the record was put there for this box by whoever runs that name's DNS. One box is one organisation: the targets of two users of the box cover each other, and the audit names the user of each check. (5) Revocation: a parent's record removed, or the parent target removed from the box (its token goes with it), covers its names no more at their next check, which runs right before every scan. A child added before its parent was verified is verified in the same transaction as the parent, with its own check message and audit row. (6) Scope does not widen: a scan of a target still lists and scans only names under that target (crt.sh, DNS, the 300-name rule of D-229), and `ScannableIP` and the IP commitment are unchanged. The executors' fence still asks for the box-bound token of the scanned target on its own name or its `_vedette-verify` name, so a deep step of a child covered only by its parent, to an address that is not the box's, is refused (fail closed) until the child has its own record. (7) A failed check names every name asked and what came back: no TXT record, another TXT value (cleaned and bounded, untrusted), a CNAME to another name (with where to put the record instead), NXDOMAIN, or a timeout. | kien 2026-11-17 (G10): git.beyondnetglobal.com is a CNAME, GoDaddy refused the TXT, and the box said only "DNS lookup failed"; he asked whether subdomains need proving again. RFC 1034 §3.6.2 (a CNAME owns no other data), hence the dedicated `_vedette-verify` label in the RFC 8552 underscore-name style that ACME's `_acme-challenge` (RFC 8555 §8.4) also uses.
