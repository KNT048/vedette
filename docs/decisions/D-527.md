(3) **Rate**: nuclei's defaults are `-rl 150 -c 25 -bs 25` (same page, Rate Limits), sized for many hosts from a big machine; a full job has one host behind the customer's own uplink and runs on an N100 next to core, so `-rl 20` (the scan module's ceiling, `MaxRate`), `-c 10` (keeps 20 a second busy up to half a second of latency), `-bs 1`, at most 200 templates a run (200 ids of at most 100 bytes stay under the 64 KiB jobs reply; with `-ms` under `nucleiout.MaxLines`), plus `-or -ot` (no request/response pair and no encoded template in a finding's line, so 200 templates matching a large page stay under the 4 MiB output cap; a deep result keeps id, name, severity, port). Arithmetic: a typical nginx port is about 2250 templates and 3170 requests: 159 s at 20 a second plus 12 runs at about 15 s to hand out and start, about 5.6 minutes; WordPress on Apache about 3330 templates and 4900 requests, about 8.3 minutes. **Bound** of the step (exposure `FullTemplatesTimeout`, instead of the 5 minutes of `TemplatesTimeout`): 10 minutes a web port (4000 templates x 1.5 requests / 20 a second = 300 s, plus 20 runs x 15 s = 300 s), 2 minutes another port, at most 90 minutes (like `GreenboneTimeout`); past it: "Deep templates did not finish within N minutes, …".
