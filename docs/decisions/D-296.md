A foreign MAC there is another machine on that address (a DHCP lease, or a static address an intruder chose): it is recorded as a device, raises the new-device alert, and the honeypot's Apply (run at once on `inventory.asset.discovered`, otherwise every minute) sees the conflict and pulls the decoy (honeypot INV-6, FORBID-1). A MAC that the cached answer lacks is asked again (`hostMACs.Fresh`, at most every 2 s) before it counts, since a decoy added a moment ago has a new macvlan MAC; a failed ask backs off for `hostNetBackoff`, so a hung host costs one timeout on the ingest path, not one per batch. Accepted trade-off: an attacker who spoofs a foreign MAC on the secondary trap IP makes a device there and the honeypot stops (it never answers on an address a device holds, INV-6); the operator gets the new-device alert, and a dedicated "device on a trap address" alert is a follow-up that needs a story.
