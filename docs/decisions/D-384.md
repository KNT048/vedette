A change is reported the way arpwatch reports a flip-flop: a WARN line and a `core_audit` row `network.gateway_mac_changed` (user `system`, target `default gateway <ip>`, detail: the new MAC and the pinned ones), at most once per 10 minutes per gateway; the row is written from its own goroutine because the report happens while response may hold the write transaction that asked for the list. No new GUI surface (no story): the row shows in Settings → Audit log under the network group. Core seeds the gateways, the resolvers and the pinned MACs (`response.HostInfra.MAC`, labelled "Default gateway of this box"); a family with no route in an answer keeps its last gateways (`keepGateways`), a gateway whose neighbour entry is missing keeps its pinned MACs.
