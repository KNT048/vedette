| D-388 | **Automatic software update: when the box checks and when it installs by itself (S-CORE-2; update-manifest §6, §12).** (1) A `core` job (`software-update`) looks every 10 minutes; a check is due 6 h ± 30 min (drawn per check) after the previous check of this process, or after the stored `last_check` when it has made none, and never at the first tick of a process (core's own update switch restarts it); it is the same check as `POST /core/updates/check` (one `update.check` row, user `system`); nothing at all while update checks are off under Privacy or before first-run setup (no install id: the administrator has not seen Privacy yet). (2) In automatic mode, after a check from the page or the job and at every tick, the release on offer is installed through the Apply run as user `system` only when: the box has a `vedette-host` (container-only never updates itself, §7); now is inside the `os.settings` maintenance window ("any time" counts) with at least 30 minutes of it left; no reboot is scheduled in the next 6 hours; no software, OS or restore run and no purge is busy; the last good check is at most 6 h 40 min old and its index not past `expires_at` (a check that fails, such as an index held back to hide a revocation, stops automatic installs: §13); and **no run to that release was made yet**. A run that failed or was reverted is not tried again automatically (the page shows its error; an administrator can press Update, or a newer release is offered), and a run that ended `done` while the box still runs the older version is not repeated. A rollback makes the mode manual (D-340 (4)); an administrator who turns automatic back on accepts the release again. (3) The "waves" of the page's hint are the rollout cohort of §6: no new rule. | Each automatic run restarts every service and core, takes a snapshot and may put the database back, so a retry loop every 10 minutes is worse than waiting for a person; checks stay at the §12 rate (no extra request to install). Sources: Fedora CoreOS Zincati checks every 5 min with jitter, updates only inside its "periodic" maintenance windows, and abandons a target after `MAX_DEPLOY_ATTEMPTS = 12` failed deploys in a row (https://coreos.github.io/zincati/usage/updates-strategy/ , https://github.com/coreos/zincati/blob/main/src/update_agent/mod.rs); its deploy attempt is a cheap staging step before reboot, ours is the whole run, hence one try. TUF freeze attack: a client must not act on metadata past its expiry (https://theupdateframework.github.io/specification/latest/).
